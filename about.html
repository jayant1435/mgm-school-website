Home / Our Story

A school with many stories.

Our school opened in 2016. Since then, it has been the children, the people and the ordinary days together that have made it special.

At home in
Nagrakata.

Manish Giri Memorial School is a local school in Nagrakata, Jalpaiguri. Our days are a mix of lessons, play, celebrations, projects and all the small things children remember.

We believe children learn well when they have room to ask, try, make mistakes, help one another and keep going.

What matters
at our school.

Learning with care

We give children time to ask questions, understand each lesson and feel supported as they learn.

Trying something new

Art, sport and outdoor activities help every child try something different and join in with confidence.

Growing together

Families and teachers work together so that each school day feels steady, encouraging and shared.

Doing good work
together.

Our school has joined Team Zindagi for community and environmental activities. Working side by side helps children see how they can care for their surroundings.

We’re glad when school life connects with the neighbourhood beyond our classrooms.

Meet Team Zindagi

Belonging is
part of learning.

We want children to feel comfortable asking questions, taking part in school activities and finding their place in a group.
