Home / Learning

A little curiosity goes a long way.

From colouring a page to asking a question at the board, there is more than one way to learn.

A fresh page.
A new idea.

Sometimes children explain what they’ve learned through their hands. A drawing, a project or a question can be the beginning of a bigger conversation.

Different ways
to learn.

From classroom questions to hands-on projects and time outside.

In class

Questions lead to ideas.

Each lesson gives children room to ask, listen and build confidence together.

Making

We learn by making things.

Small projects help children explore, create and understand ideas with their hands.

Outdoors

Learning with nature.

Gardening moments help children notice growth, practise patience and care for living things.

Ask them what surprised them.

A simple question at the end of a school day can lead to a lovely conversation at home.
